Gig Cue keeps your show within reach when lights go down.

Build ordered setlists from your own song cards. Save each song's key, tempo and short stage cues, and place breaks exactly where they belong. In stage mode, see the current song and what comes next, with large, deliberate navigation controls for busy hands. Save multiple shows on your iPhone. Preview a JSON backup before replacing saved shows, or share a plain-text setlist with your bandmates using the iPhone share sheet.

Gig Cue works offline. Shows and private cues stay on your iPhone until you choose to share or export them. No account, music subscription, microphone, location access, lyrics scraping or tracking. Built for readable, accessible use on stage.
